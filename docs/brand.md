# Paryx Brand

**Name:** Paryx  
**Pronunciation:** PAR-icks  
**Primary tagline:** The platform behind your club.

## Product family

- **Paryx** — master brand
- **Paryx Club** — staff / club-management workspace
- **Paryx Member** — future golfer-facing application
- **Paryx Pay** — reserved for future payments capability
- **Paryx Edge** — reserved for future optional local/offline service

## Core palette

- Deep green: `#064831`
- Deep green dark: `#022D1D`
- Light gold: `#E5C45F`
- Highlight gold: `#F4D667`
- Warm gold tint: `#F8F0D9`

Gold is an accent, not the dominant interface colour. The staff workspace should remain clean, restrained and software-led.
